# Build Your Own AI Chatbot with Zapier

## Overview
In this activity, you will create a helpful **AI Chatbot** using **Zapier Chatbots**. Your chatbot must have a **specific "brain" (role and task)** designed to solve a real problem or help users complete a specific task.

You are free to choose your own topic and **create your own dataset files** (such as `.docx`, `.pdf`, `.csv`, `.txt`, or any document format your dataset needs). Your chatbot must also **collect information from the user** using AI logic, and you will submit your finished chatbot link by **opening a GitHub Issue**.

---

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this activity, you will be able to:
1. Design a clear purpose and problem-solving task for an AI chatbot.
2. Create and organize custom dataset files for a chatbot's knowledge base.
3. Configure the **5 required chatbot settings** in Zapier Chatbots.
4. Set up **AI logic** to gather useful information from users during a chat.
5. Publish your chatbot link and submit it properly via a **GitHub Issue**.

---

## Step 1: Choose Your Chatbot Brain & Problem to Solve
Pick a clear role and task for your chatbot. For example:
* **Study & Lab Helper:** Guides learners step-by-step, gives hints, and checks understanding without just giving away answers.
* **Troubleshooting & Triage Helper:** Asks questions to find out what is wrong, checks known fixes, and summarizes the issue when escalation is needed.
* **Project & Workflow Helper:** Turns rough notes or ideas into structured plans, checklists, or templates.

You can also create your own topic as long as it solves a clear problem or supports a specific task.

---

## Step 2: Create Your Own Dataset Files
Before or while setting up your chatbot, **create your own dataset files** based on the topic your chatbot will handle.

* You can use **Word (`.docx`), PDF (`.pdf`), CSV (`.csv`), text files (`.txt`), or other supported document files**.
* Make sure your files are clean, organized, and easy to read (use clear headings and labels).
* Do not just copy-paste random text—write or organize data that actually helps your chatbot answer questions, give hints, follow rules, or solve problems.

---

## Step 3: Configure the 5 Required Settings in Zapier Chatbots
Open **Zapier Chatbots**, create your chatbot, and configure all **5 items below** carefully:

1. **Instruction**
   * Tell the chatbot who it is, what problem it solves, how it should talk to users, and what rules it must follow (for example: stay on topic, give hints first, or keep answers clear and polite).
2. **Knowledge**
   * Upload the custom dataset files you created (Word, PDF, CSV, etc.) so your chatbot can use your specific information as its knowledge brain.
3. **Logic**
   * Set up the **AI logic** so your chatbot **gets information from the user** (such as their name, concern/topic, level or details, or specific answers needed to solve their problem) and uses or records that information properly.
4. **Theme**
   * Customize the look and feel of your chatbot (such as colors, bot name, welcome message, and appearance) so it matches your chatbot's purpose.
5. **AI Model**
   * Select the **AI Model** for your chatbot and make sure it works smoothly with your instructions and knowledge files.

---

## Step 4: Test Your Chatbot
Before submitting, test your chatbot from start to finish:
* Ask questions that test your **Knowledge** files.
* Check if your **Logic** asks for and captures the needed **information from the user**.
* Make sure your **Instruction**, **Theme**, and **AI Model** work together without errors.

---

## Step 5: Submit Your Chatbot Link via a GitHub Issue
Once your chatbot is ready and tested:
1. Copy the **shareable link** to your Zapier Chatbot.
2. Go to this repository's **Issues** tab on GitHub and click **New Issue**.
3. Use a clear issue title, such as:
   `[Chatbot Submission] Your Full Name - Your Chatbot Title`
4. In the issue description, include:
   * **Your Name & Section:**
   * **Chatbot Name & Purpose / Problem Solved:**
   * **Dataset Files Created (and file types):**
   * **What Information Your AI Logic Gets from Users:**
   * **Chatbot Link:** `paste-your-zapier-chatbot-link-here`
5. Click **Submit new issue**.
